About Me

I’m Lingesh Kumar M, a Generative AI and Machine Learning Developer based in Bengaluru, currently pursuing my Master of Computer Applications with a specialization in Generative AI. I enjoy building intelligent systems that combine large language models, AI Agents, retrieval-augmented generation, NLP, machine learning and full-stack engineering. My work focuses on turning AI from something that simply generates responses into systems that can understand context, reason over information, interact with tools and contribute to real-world workflows. From autonomous personal assistants and multilingual voice systems to AI-powered race strategy, document intelligence and interview platforms, I’m interested in building practical, Real-world AI products that connect intelligent models with real applications.

LEO 2.0

Autonomous personal AI computer assistant
